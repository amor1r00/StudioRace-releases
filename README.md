# Studio Race Beta

Free downloads of Studio Race and Race Engineer HUD for Assetto Corsa.

Race Engineer HUD is an in-game app (CSP Lua): engineer calls on the radio, a Tyre Lab with live tyre heat, a 3D racing line with brake points, a corner-by-corner coach, challenges and a ghost car. Studio Race is the desktop app that installs it and keeps your sessions, laps and setups. It also has an AI line builder that works out a faster line for your car on a track.

## Download

Get the latest installer from the [Releases page](https://github.com/amor1r00/StudioRace-releases/releases/latest): `StudioRace_Beta_<version>_Setup.exe`.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer is not code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

You need Assetto Corsa with Content Manager and Custom Shaders Patch (0.2.0 or newer), on Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Once installed, Studio Race updates itself from here. This repository only holds the downloads; the source code is not public.

## Help and ideas

Join the Race Engineer HUD Discord: the help forum for bugs, suggestions for ideas.

Terms of use and privacy policy are shown when you install, and in the app under Settings, Terms and privacy. In short: free, no account, no tracking, and your laps stay on your PC.
